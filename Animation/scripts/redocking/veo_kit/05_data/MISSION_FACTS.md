"ARISE deploys a fleet of eight autonomous CubeSats from a single carrier spacecraft.
Flying in formation, they intercept asteroid Apophis during its 2029 Earth flyby. In a
two-second window at closest approach the fleet fires 136 synchronised camera
exposures, strobe-lighting even the asteroid's dark side to map 99 percent of its
surface in stereo. The deputies then fly themselves home, and a reinforcement-learning
policy docks all eight back onto the carrier — with no human in the loop."
